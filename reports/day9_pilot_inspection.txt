DAY 9 PILOT — MANUAL SANITY INSPECTION
========================================================================
Failure rate proves the pipeline is mechanically sound. It does
NOT prove the extractions are GOOD. Read these by eye: does the
attack_vector / component / impact actually match the description?
A model can return flawless JSON full of nonsense.

Note: ground truth below uses NVD's UPPER_CASE enum (e.g. NETWORK,
HIGH); the model is prompted for Title Case (Network, High). That
casing gap is handled at Day 18 scoring, not here — don't mistake
it for a wrong answer during inspection.
========================================================================
